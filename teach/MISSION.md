# Mission: Reliable engineering delivery with AI Agents

## Why

Build the judgment and practical skill to take backend and AI-enabled systems from a real need to reliable delivery, using AI Agents as engineering collaborators while retaining responsibility for requirements, design, review, and evidence of quality.

## Success looks like
- Turn ambiguous needs into explicit scenarios, contracts, constraints, and acceptance evidence.
- Design and defend proportionate architectures and technology choices, including failure modes and operational tradeoffs.
- Direct AI Agents effectively, inspect their changes critically, and integrate work with clear tests and verification.
- Deliver, diagnose, and improve backend and AI-enabled systems with evidence for correctness and reliability.

## Constraints
- Use the existing Go project as context; do not duplicate its progress state in this workspace.
- Teach the capability needed for the current problem, from language details through architecture and operations.
- Adjust lesson and practice size to problem difficulty and learning value; do not require long sessions or exercises by default.
- Distinguish exposure, implementation, verification, understanding, and transfer. Do not infer mastery from generated code or passing tests alone.

## Out of scope
- Requiring the learner to handwrite all implementation without AI assistance.
- Mechanical long sessions or exercises that do not improve the current capability.
